Rice Product Guide

Basmati Rice:
Long grain aromatic rice commonly used for biryani and pulao dishes.

Sona Masoori Rice:
Medium grain lightweight rice commonly used in South Indian cooking.

Brown Rice:
Whole grain rice with higher fiber and nutritional value.

Ponni Rice:
Popular rice variety widely consumed in Tamil Nadu and South India.

Idli Rice:
Rice specially processed for preparing idli and dosa batter.

Boiled Rice:
Partially boiled rice with improved shelf life and nutritional retention.

Organic Rice:
Rice cultivated without harmful chemicals and pesticides.

Storage Instructions:

* Store in airtight containers
* Keep away from moisture
* Avoid direct sunlight
* Use within recommended shelf life

Cooking Tips:

* Wash rice before cooking
* Use appropriate water ratio
* Soak rice for better texture
* Store cooked rice properly

Nutritional Benefits:
Rice provides carbohydrates, energy, and essential nutrients.

Premium Packaging:
Products are packed hygienically to preserve freshness and quality.

Shelf Life:
Most rice products have shelf life between 6 months and 12 months depending on storage conditions.
