Shipping Charges:

Orders below ₹999 may include delivery charges.

Order Tracking:
Tracking details are provided after shipment.

Delivery Attempts:
Delivery partners may attempt delivery up to three times.

Failed Delivery:
Orders returned due to incorrect address or unreachable customer may require additional delivery charges.

Products are packed using moisture-resistant packaging to maintain freshness.

International Shipping:
Currently unavailable.

Holiday Delays:
During festivals and holidays, deliveries may take longer than usual.

Customers are advised to ensure accurate address and phone details during checkout.
