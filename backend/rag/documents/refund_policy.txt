Refund Policy

Customers are eligible for refunds under the following conditions:

* Damaged product received
* Incorrect product delivered
* Missing items in package
* Delivery failure caused by logistics issues
* Duplicate payment

Refund requests must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery.

Refund Process:

1. Customer submits complaint.
2. Support team verifies issue.
3. Refund approval is processed.
4. Amount is credited within 5 to 7 business days.

Refund Modes:

* Original payment source
* Bank transfer
* Wallet refund

Non-Refundable Situations:

* Product opened or used
* Incorrect address provided by customer
* Delayed complaints beyond 48 hours
* Minor packaging damage without product damage

Replacement Policy:
Products may be replaced instead of refunded depending on stock availability.

Cancellation Refunds:
Cancelled prepaid orders are refunded within 3 to 5 business days.

Cash on Delivery Orders:
COD orders are eligible only for replacement unless approved by support management.

The company reserves the right to reject fraudulent refund requests.
