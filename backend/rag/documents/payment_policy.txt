Payment Policy

The platform supports secure online payments.

Accepted Payment Methods:

* UPI
* Google Pay
* PhonePe
* Paytm
* Credit Cards
* Debit Cards
* Net Banking
* Cash on Delivery

Payment Security:
All transactions are processed through secure payment gateways with encryption.

Failed Payments:
If money is deducted but order is not confirmed, the amount is usually refunded automatically within 5 to 7 business days.

Partial Payments:
Partial payment options may be available during promotional offers.

Invoice:
Digital invoices are automatically generated after successful orders.

Cash on Delivery:
COD availability depends on customer location and order amount.

Fraud Detection:
Suspicious transactions may be blocked automatically for security reasons.

Refund Timelines:
UPI Refunds: 2 to 5 business days
Card Refunds: 5 to 7 business days
Net Banking Refunds: 3 to 7 business days

Customers should contact support if refund delays exceed expected timelines.
